Research Work

My published research on the intersection of AI and human emotion.

Original Research Article · JSIAR, Vol. 1, Issue 1 · Pages 97–106 · April 2025

Feeling the Pulse: Unveiling Human Emotion through the Lens of Multimodal Big Data

Yash Kumar & Kanishka Shashi — Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Noida International University, Greater Noida, India

This paper reviews how combining text, voice, and facial-expression data helps AI systems recognize human emotion more reliably than single-signal approaches. It looks at the technical and ethical challenges involved — from syncing different data streams to protecting privacy and avoiding bias — and examines how these systems are being applied in healthcare, education, and marketing, while calling for more transparent and responsible emotion-AI design.
